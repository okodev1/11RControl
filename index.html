Eleven Rack MIDI Architecture & Deep Editing Guide

1. Virtual Rig Cheat Mode (Base PC + Full CC Auto-Push)

Standard MIDI 1.0 cannot hot-swap DSP models (Amp, Cab, FX1/FX2 type) via CC alone—it recalls models via Program Change (PC 0–103). To let you build complete custom Rigs on the interface, Auto-Push Mode performs a 2-stage MIDI burst whenever you switch Rigs, load a saved Rig, or pick an Amp: 1) It sends Bank + PC for the slot's Base HW Patch to load the underlying Amp/Cab/FX models, and 2) 85ms later, it automatically blasts all 10 Block Bypasses and all Knob Parameter CCs across every block so the hardware snaps to your exact web interface Rig!

2. Page 1 & Page 2 Deep Parameter Editing (Table 12 CCs)

Every multi-parameter block (AMP, DIST, FX 1, FX 2, MOD, DELAY, REVERB) now supports PAGE 1 (Settings 1–6) and PAGE 2 (Settings 7–12) mapped to the official Eleven Rack User Guide Table 12 MIDI Continuous Controllers:

  • Amp Settings 1–10: CCs 14, 15, 16, 17, 21, 10, 113, 3, 85, 24
  • Distortion Settings 1–6: CCs 28, 79, 80, 81, 82, 83
  • FX 1 Settings 1–9: CCs 20, 42, 60, 77, 117, 118, 119, 5
  • FX 2 Settings 1–9: CCs 114, 115, 116, 97, 98, 99, 37, 46
  • Modulation Settings 1–7: CCs 62, 52, 53, 54, 57, 51, 56
  • Delay Settings 1–8: CCs 71, 72, 73, 86, 87, 34, 48, 49
  • Reverb Settings 1–6: CCs 74, 75, 76, 39, 77, 41

3. Preset Names on the LCD Screen

Standard MIDI 1.0 (PC/CC) does not transmit ASCII preset names from the Eleven Rack hardware. To give you a clear, prominent readout, the LCD Header and 4 Rig Footswitches come pre-populated with descriptive Rig titles for User (`A1–Z4`) and Factory (`a1–z4`) banks. Click the Rig Name directly on the LCD screen at any time to type your own custom Rig name for that slot—it saves automatically to your browser!

4. How Two-Way MIDI Sync Works (PUSH vs. LIVE RX)

Sending (PUSH TO 11R): Clicking PUSH TO 11R transmits all current UI knob positions and effect bypass states to the Eleven Rack so the hardware immediately matches your screen.

Receiving (MIDI IN): Standard MIDI cannot request a full state dump on demand, however if you select Eleven Rack in MIDI IN as well as MIDI OUT, whenever you turn a physical knob, toggle an effect button, or scroll Rigs on the front panel of your Eleven Rack, the hardware transmits those `CC` and `PC` messages over USB and this web interface updates its knobs, bypass LEDs, and active Rig slot in real time!
